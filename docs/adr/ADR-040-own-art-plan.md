# ADR-040 — Own art plan: sources, build tools and provenance

**Status:** Accepted (coordinator, 2026-10-25, under the authority the owner delegated). Roadmap M6. Builds on ADR-007 (own art pipeline, D5), ADR-004
(own-art provenance), ADR-005 (3/4 view), ADR-006 (layered characters), ADR-031 D9 (release gate, removed by ADR-042 P7) and ADR-039 (audio).
Condensed on 2026-10-26: the phase log and the references to the early content are removed.

## Context

Every art file the game loads is Nosum's own: drawn in code, made with a generator and checked by hand, or cut from a CC0 pack. This ADR records
where the art comes from, which tools build it, and where each file's provenance is kept.

## Decision

- **D1 — Art direction.** 16 × 16 px tiles on a 16 px grid, 3/4 top-down (ADR-005). Characters and tall objects use 16 × 32 frames, drawn at a
  whole-number scale with smoothing off (ADR-007 D1). One shared palette of at most 48 colours for every file. Objects, characters and animals have a
  1 px outline in a darker tone of their own fill, never pure black. Ground and terrain tiles have no outline, so they tile seamlessly. Light comes from
  the top left; shadows are part of the sprite.
- **D2 — Sources.** Each file takes one or more of these routes:
  - **PixelLab** (AI-generated): tilesets, objects, panels and character sets. The prompt, seed and date of each generation are recorded next to the
    file. PixelLab's terms and the ownership of generated art are checked before release.
  - **Hand pixel work** by the owner or an artist, for layered farmer parts that must line up pixel for pixel, and for animation frames that keep an
    exact silhouette.
  - **Commissioned art** under a work-for-hire or full-assignment contract.
  - **CC0 packs only**, with no attribution duty and commercial use allowed. A pack is recorded with its licence text in `docs/art/licenses/`.
  - **Fonts:** VT323, SIL OFL. **Audio:** ADR-039, Nosum's own synthesised sounds and music.
- **D3 — Build tools.**
  - `tools/art_repack` rebuilds the sheets under `assets/` from CC0 packs and from pictures drawn in code.
  - `tools/Nosum.MapGen` writes the own maps under `art/own/Maps/`: it compiles the text layouts into `.tmx` files, generates the mine floors and
    draws the own tilesheets.
  - The PixelLab corner tilesets used for map terrain are kept under `art/own/source/pixellab`, with their metadata.
- **D4 — Provenance records.**
  - [`docs/art/provenance.md`](../art/provenance.md) lists every file under `assets/`, its package, its sources and what it shows. It is generated by
    `tools/art_repack` and is not edited by hand.
  - [`art/own/SOURCES.md`](../../art/own/SOURCES.md) lists every own map and tilesheet with its source.
  - `ProvenanceTests` checks the provenance record.

## Alternatives considered

- **Replace every file under `assets/`:** most files are never loaded, so only the loaded set is replaced.
- **Repack into atlases (ADR-004):** the code addresses sheets by index (ADR-007 D2), so repacking would change code for no gain.
- **Re-grid to 32 px:** doubles the cell count and changes every table in the code.
- **Only open-licensed packs:** no pack matches the sheet layouts and the farmer's layers. Packs stay as one route in D2.

## Consequences

- Each file has a recorded source, and each route has its own check. AI-generated art needs a terms check before a commercial release.
- A new sheet or map is listed in its provenance record in the same change.
- The palette and outline rules of D1 apply to every generator and hand edit.

## Revisit when

The owner chooses the production route for a new package, the sound list of ADR-039 changes, or PixelLab's terms change.
