# Sourcing the map tilesets, 2026-10-26

Question: can an open-licensed 16 x 16 top-down pack replace the code-drawn map sheets (`art/own/Maps/*.png`, ADR-042 D3), or should the code-drawn
sheets be improved? Acceptance rule: CC0, or CC-BY 3.0/4.0 with the attribution recorded; no GPL-only, "non-commercial" or unclear license; nothing that
copies or imitates the early content's game. The pack must also fit the sheets' fixed grids: the compiled maps and the `.tileset` trait files address
tiles by index (terrain: 28 fills and 98 corner-transition tiles; nature: 2 x 3 tree blocks; interior: 2 x 2 bed and table), so a pack tile can only
replace a tile if a repack step can place it at that index.

"Verified" below means the page was fetched on 2026-10-26 and the license text read on the page (or inside the archive); "search only" means the license
was taken from a search result and not checked, so the pack is not used on that basis.

## Result

No candidate that passes the license rule covers the sheets' grids, and the one with the best art (Adventure Awaits) covers one scene, not seven
terrain pairs, trees in three species, a town, two interiors and a cave. A mixed result would put detailed soft-shaded tiles beside flat outlined ones
from `assets/` on the same map. So the sheets are improved in code (ADR-042 D3), which keeps every index and the traits:

- **Terrain.** Grass in five tones from smooth tileable noise with blade tufts; dirt with patches and pebbles; sand with ripples; cobbles as rounded
  stones with their own tones; cliff as courses of lit blocks; water with swells, glint lines and sparkles. The 98 transition tiles now use noise that
  repeats every 16 pixels and does not depend on the corner code, so neighbours join without a seam, and each pair has its own rim: a fringe and
  shadow for grass over dirt, sand or cobbles, wet sand and foam at the shore, a muddy bank, a lit lip over the cliff.
- **Nature.** Trees with lobed canopies (light from the top left, a dark rim where lobes overlap, leaf flecks, one outline), a barked trunk with
  roots and a checkered ground shadow; pines in four ragged tiers; shaded bushes with berries or blossom; faceted rocks; a stump with rings; rails and
  posts with grain and a shadow; a hedge that runs on past the tile edge.
- **Town, interior, cave.** Mottled plaster, timber beams, glazed windows with a sill, plank doors, shingles with lit tops and dark joints, brick with
  tones, ashlar, awnings with a scalloped hem; board floors with per-board tones, a woven rug, a quilted bed, grained furniture; cave floor patches,
  rock walls with a lit rim, crystals and a dithered torch glow.

A before and after contact sheet is in [`docs/qa/`](../qa/) and in-game screenshots of five maps are in `docs/qa/maps/`.

Reproducible: `dotnet run --project tools/Nosum.MapGen -- sheets --check` exits 1 when a committed sheet differs from what the code draws.

If an open pack that fits later turns up, the repack route stays open: write `tools/` code that copies each tile to its index, check it with a `--check`
flag, keep the pack under `art/source/` when its license allows (CC0 does), and list it in `art/own/SOURCES.md` and `provenance.md`.
