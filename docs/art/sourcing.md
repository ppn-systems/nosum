# Sourcing the non-terrain art from CC0 packs

Decision input for the owner's PixelLab budget ([ADR-040](../adr/ADR-040-own-art-plan.md), packages 1 and 3–9; terrain is another branch). Each candidate
was downloaded into an empty directory outside the repository, its license read inside the archive (OpenGameArt: on the asset page) and its sheets
opened as images only. A pack is **rejected** on any license other than CC0, however well it fits. The art direction is ADR-040 D1: a 16 px grid,
3/4 top-down, warm farm look, a 1 px outline in a darker tone of the fill.

Work in progress: this page is completed with the plan per package when the repack is finished.
